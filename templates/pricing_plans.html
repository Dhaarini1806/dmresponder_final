Understanding DMResponder Pricing Plans & DM Limit Reset

Many users get confused about how DM limits and billing dates work. This guide explains everything step-by-step with simple examples and a chart for easy understanding.

Overview

When you subscribe to a DMResponder plan (like Boost, Pro, or Max), you get access to:

  • Advanced DM automation features (Comment to DM, Follow-Gated DMs, Flow Automation, etc.)
  • A monthly DM limit (like 40K DMs for the Boost plan)
  • Access validity for your chosen billing cycle (monthly or yearly)

However, there are two important things to understand:

  • Your DM limit resets on the 1st of every month, not on your plan purchase date.
  • Your plan renewal date stays fixed based on when you purchased the plan.

Example: How DM Limit and Renewal Work

Suppose you purchase the Boost Plan (40,000 DMs/month) on 25th January.

Date What Happens DM Limit
25 Jan You subscribe to the Boost plan and get 40,000 DMs instantly. 40,000 DMs available
25–31 Jan You can use these 40K DMs during the remaining days of January. (Balance reduces as you send)
1 Feb Your DM limit resets automatically to 40,000 again for February. New 40,000 DMs
25 Feb Your plan renews automatically (payment date), but your DM limit does not reset again here—it stays the same because it already reset on Feb 1. (No change)
1 Mar Your DM limit resets again for March. New 40,000 DMs

In Simple Words

  • You get your DM limit immediately when you first subscribe.
  • Then, your DM limit resets automatically on the 1st of every month (not your renewal date).
  • Your billing/renewal date only affects payment and plan validity, not your DM count.

Cancelled Plans

  • You'll still have access to your plan features until your billing cycle ends.
  • You can continue using your remaining DMs during that time.
  • Once expired, all premium features and DM sending ability are paused.

Common Questions

Q: Why didn't my DM limit renew on my billing date?

A: Because DM limits refresh on the 1st of each month, not when you pay. Your billing date only controls payment and plan validity.

Q: If I buy a plan on the 28th, do I lose days?

A: No. You get full DMs instantly and another full reset on the 1st. That means you get a few extra DMs in your first few days.

Q: What if I cancel right after buying?

A: You can still use your DMs until your plan expires. After that, automation stops.
