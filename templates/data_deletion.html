Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Data Deletion Policy

DMResponder respects your privacy and provides you with the ability to request deletion of your personal data in accordance with applicable data protection regulations.

2. What Data Can Be Deleted

You can request deletion of:

  • Your account and profile information
  • Leads and contact information collected through automations
  • Conversation history and messages
  • Automation workflows and execution logs
  • Analytics and usage data

3. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as necessary to provide our services. Upon deletion request, we will remove your data within 30 days, except where required by law to retain it.

4. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data:

  1. Log in to your DMResponder account
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  3. Follow the confirmation process
  4. Or email us at privacy@dmresponder.com with your request

5. Meta App Data

If you've connected your Instagram account, you can also request data deletion through Meta's app settings. DMResponder will not retain your Instagram data after you disconnect your account.

6. Verification

We may require verification of your identity before processing your deletion request to ensure security and prevent unauthorized data deletion.

7. Exceptions

We may retain certain data if required by law, for legal proceedings, or to maintain service integrity. We will notify you of any such exceptions.

8. Contact Us

For data deletion requests or inquiries, contact us at: privacy@dmresponder.com
